Unit sprites (knight, archer, guard, scout)
===========================================

Composited from the Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) Universal Spritesheet:
https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator

Source art by wulax, Redshrike, and other LPC contributors.

License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 (see upstream CREDITS.csv)
https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/blob/master/CREDITS.csv

You must credit the LPC contributors when distributing this game.
Layered body, armour, weapon, and shield sprites were composited into
single south-facing unit frames and scaled 2× for battlefield use.

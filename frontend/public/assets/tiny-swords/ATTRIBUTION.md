# Tiny Swords

Tiny Swords is created by Pixel Frog and is available for free from:
https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords

The free pack may be used in personal and commercial projects, modified as
needed, and does not require attribution. The original assets may not be
redistributed, resold, or repackaged separately from a project.

The files in `generated/` are runtime-ready crops made from the original
spritesheets for Phaser card art and particle emitters.
